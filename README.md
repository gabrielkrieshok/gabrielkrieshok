# Gabriel Krieshok

Technology leader and tinkerer, recently relocated from Washington, DC to Minneapolis. Most recently I led Client Technology at RTI International (2022–2026): a 25-person team covering cloud platforms, AI governance, and federal compliance across a $1B+ public sector research portfolio. Before that: global digital technologies at CARE, digital transformation and an AI R&D lab at Abt Associates, and five years building digital programs at the U.S. Peace Corps.

These days I'm settling into the Twin Cities and building things on the side.
